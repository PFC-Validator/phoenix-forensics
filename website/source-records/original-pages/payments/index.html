Sources & method

Receipt evidence

Each received row opens its dated receipt events, amounts, full wallet addresses and transaction links. A single transaction can contain multiple receipt events. The original grant was executed at block 3,410,102 and has no ordinary transaction hash.

Historical USD

Values use the preserved historical market references, generally the preceding completed hour, or documented redemption references. They are estimates of value at receipt, not actual sale proceeds. USDC is valued from market references rather than assumed to equal $1.

Coverage

This is a dated record snapshot, not a live balance or complete lifetime statement. The underlying receipt register runs to 11 October 2026, with a targeted stewardship update on 17 October. Project custody, gross receipts and personal retained earnings are different measures.
