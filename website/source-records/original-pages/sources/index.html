Sources and verification

Original transactions, proposals, public statements and supporting records, organized by subject.

  • Control history: searchable register, source index, checksums.
  • Payments: transaction ledger, receipt records and transaction links, payment schedule.
  • Contract authority: dated timeline, contract inventory, repository preservation and verification.
  • Public records are preserved in multiple online copies and archives.
  • Supporting records: public source index.
  • Forum: original discussion and independent whole-thread archive.

Verify with an AI tool or your own scripts

The narrative and source records are available without JavaScript. The interactive filters and transaction viewer use JavaScript. Use the plain-text starting index, structured verification index, or full history text. The index links to complete data, original sources and file hashes.

Validator reports are labelled separately from public transaction records and public statements. Reporting-source identities and correspondence are excluded.

How to check a claim

  1. Follow the claim’s source link to its transaction, proposal, statement or document.
  2. Check the event date and the date the source was captured.
  3. Follow the original source links; use the manifest to compare preserved file hashes.

Preserved originals and analytical summaries are identified separately. A checksum verifies matching bytes; independent captures and source records provide separate evidence of publication and timing. Forum usernames identify participants in the discussion.

Figures can be checked against the linked transaction records and datasets.
