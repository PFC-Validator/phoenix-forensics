## Product Purpose

Help holders assess governance independence across the Phoenix board, Think Tank, ERIS signers and contracts, validators, contractors and large voting accounts. ## Editorial approach

Write for a LUNA holder deciding whether independent review is warranted. Use direct descriptions of votes, payments and permissions. Remove slogans, repeated introductions and defensive asides. Explain a technical distinction where it changes the interpretation; keep query logs and reproduction details in the source library. Preserve amounts, dates, counterevidence and material uncertainty.

The lead payment example is the ERIS/Creda grant: A209 enabled 200,000 USDC for its first three milestones; A215 enabled 10,000 USDC for analytics. 0xphilipp proposed and joined Vini and MB in both approvals. Distinguish those milestone releases from the original grant award and from Vini’s separate 16,900-USDC audit payment.

The voting page must explain the consequences of passing 4853 before the tally. Cover the express removal-facilitation request, the limits and tension in bylaws Articles 2.4.5.4 and 2.6.2, and Article 1.10’s veto exception. Explain the route following failed A272 and the proposer’s responses. Distinguish possible removal from automatic removal, and three-of-three at an unchanged 75% threshold from two-of-three at 60%. Preserve Rose’s dissent, Vini’s own payment interest, the conditional nature of concentration risk and the offered off-chain voting commitment.
