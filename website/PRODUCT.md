## Users

LUNA token holders on the Terra blockchain, especially large holders.

## Product Purpose

Help holders assess governance independence across the Phoenix board, Think Tank, ERIS signers and contracts, validators, contractors and large voting accounts. The immediate purpose is a credible and impartial proposal 4853 process; the longer-term purpose is structural governance independent of any one stakeholder.

Preserve figures, dates, caveats, supporting files and evidence links; do not adopt the original site’s narrative as an independent finding.

Maintain a neutral, evidence-focused editorial standard. Do not publish personal attacks, character judgments, unsupported allegations of dishonesty or motive, calls for unwanted contact, or harassment. Apply this standard to the mirrored source presentation as well as the main briefing. Curate relevant records rather than reproducing accusatory commentary. ## Product Principles

- Connect funding decisions to project beneficiaries and governance authority where the records establish those relationships.
- Let readers follow material statements to the underlying evidence.
- Preserve accounting qualifications and dated coverage when simplifying explanations.
- Prioritize comprehension for large LUNA holders.
- Keep the presentation calm and spacious; sensational framing and dense pages would feel wrong to the user.
