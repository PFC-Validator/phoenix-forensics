## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Published with Sites at https://phoenix-foundation-irregularities.pfc.zone, separately from the original website.

## Users

LUNA token holders on the Terra blockchain, especially large holders.

## Product Purpose

Help holders examine Philipp's funding of his own projects and the oversight around those decisions. The user's central concern is that chain leaders fund their own projects without oversight; the agreed scope is Philipp, rather than multiple leaders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Rebuild https://philipp-payments.pages.dev/ using Impeccable and Diagram Design. Rewrite and reorganize the existing content for clarity. Preserve figures, dates, caveats, supporting source files and evidence links. Keep the original website intact and publish the rebuild separately.

Use the public handle 0xphilipp in authored page copy, diagrams and metadata (verified as @0xphilipp on X). Do not introduce a profile link or personal-identification appendix. Retained raw financial records may preserve their original public labels; do not alter transaction data to relabel them. Omit surname references, identity-disclosure sections and links whose purpose is to establish his personal identity. Apply this to the adapted source pages, readable versions, downloads and indexes as well as the redesigned interface. Preserve relevant transaction and funding evidence, and disclose privacy edits rather than describing changed copies as byte-identical originals.

Distinguish sourced facts, interpretations and unanswered questions. Treat the user's concern about oversight as the question to investigate, rather than an established finding independent of evidence. Preserve distinctions between gross project funding, receipts, locked allocations and personal profit, as well as paid and conditional unpaid amounts.

Maintain a neutral, evidence-focused editorial standard. Do not publish personal attacks, character judgments, unsupported allegations of dishonesty or motive, calls for unwanted contact, or harassment. Apply this standard to the mirrored source presentation as well as the main briefing. Curate relevant records rather than reproducing accusatory commentary. Preserve material explanations and evidence limits.

## Evidence on Hand

The published original website is the source corpus. Its pages, public evidence files and receipt ledgers have been collected and retained with the rebuild. Key proposal, vote and payment records have been inspected for the diagrams; this remains a rewritten presentation rather than an independent audit of every underlying transaction. Original source code was not supplied.

## Product Principles

- Connect funding decisions to project beneficiaries and governance authority where the records establish those relationships.
- Let readers follow material statements to the underlying evidence.
- Preserve accounting qualifications and dated coverage when simplifying explanations.
- Prioritize comprehension for large LUNA holders.
- Keep the presentation calm and spacious; sensational framing and dense pages would feel wrong to the user.

## Design Workflow

Build directly and publish an early working version for others to give feedback. The user prioritizes a shareable website sooner over reviewing image mockups before coding.

## Confirmed Design Direction

Holder briefing, selected by the user: calm white and deep-green presentation, short overview, clear funding and governance diagrams, and supporting records available on demand. The user explicitly approved matching the diagrams to this direction. Build directly; no image mockup approval round.

The user subsequently requested stronger visual appeal, diagrams showing Philipp's beneficiary participation in approval, and timeline motion graphics. Retain the calm tone and factual boundaries while using a more substantial forest-green visual field, selectable documented cases and optional controlled timeline playback. All facts remain available without animation or JavaScript, with a complete reduced-motion alternative.

The homepage must clearly present four concerns with evidence links and adjacent caveats: beneficiary participation in approvals; retained technical authority beyond stated handover dates; milestone acceptance evidence; and A272’s proposed concentration of approval power. A272 failed; no inference of motive or dishonesty follows from the sequence alone.
