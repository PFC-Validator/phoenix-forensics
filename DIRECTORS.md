# Directors and related governance wallets

**Scope:** Phoenix board members MB, Vini, and Rose, plus Rebel’s separate Think Tank/validator relationships. No identity research or surname attribution.

## Main findings

- The other directors do not all vote alike. **Rose has the most independent record**; Vini also has explicit NO/ABSTAIN examples; MB is highly aligned with Philipp but has five differing shared ballots across the combined wallet history.
- **Vini is the only one of these three who also holds an ERIS fixed-multisig seat** (10 of 90 weight).
- **Rebel is not in the Phoenix board or ERIS fixed multisig.** Rebel has a separate Think Tank veto seat, no ballots on its five proposals, and a confirmed 7,500-USDC invoice payment from the ERIS protocol fee account.
- Beneficiary participation is broader than Philipp: Vini proposed and voted on A249; Vini and Rose voted on A159 containing their own recurring compensation; MB had no A159 ballot.
- The earlier Estima A258 was rejected by Vini and Rose. A reduced A266 was then approved by Vini, Rose, and MB without Philipp. This is counterevidence to permanent lockstep.

## Director-specific records

### MB

- Combined history: 252 YES and one NO in 253 ballots.
- Current-wallet history is entirely YES; earlier wallet contains the A28 technical-correction NO.
- No proposal in the preserved A1–A289 set was authored by MB.
- No current or earlier MB wallet has a native 4853 ballot.
- Treasury in the 2026 coverage window: **57,096 USDC**, including ten 5,640-USDC compensation claims and 696 USDC. Two same-day claims were separate entitlements inside action 100, not an unexplained duplicate.
- A125 documents replacement of the earlier associated wallet by the current board wallet on 27 March 2025. This supports role continuity, not exclusive key custody.
- Preserved Neutron records describe beneficiary participation in Superbolt distributions. Those are separate-chain records and should not be added to Phoenix Treasury totals.

### Vini

- 112 YES, two NO, one ABSTAIN in 115 ballots.
- NO on A55 and A258; ABSTAIN on A189.
- Authored A249, a 16,900-USDC security-audit payment to Vini’s own address, and joined its minimum passing YES set. Rose and MB supplied the other two YES votes; Philipp had no recorded ballot.
- A272 targeted Vini’s board seat. Vini did not vote; Rose voted NO; Philipp and MB voted YES, so it failed.
- Vini and Rose had voted NO on the earlier Estima A258. A reduced A266 was approved by Vini, Rose, and MB, with Philipp absent.
- Vini holds a separate **10-of-90 ERIS fixed-multisig weight**. Across the nine ERIS proposals, Vini has eight YES ballots and no ballot on proposal 8. Proposal 9 transferred two contract admins to the Phoenix board after A272.
- Treasury in the 2026 coverage window: **66,480 USDC**, including nine 5,000-USDC compensation payments, the 16,900-USDC audit payment, and recurring infra payments.
- October actions 118–120 created new Stewardship payments for Philipp, Rose, and MB, but not Vini. Separate infra actions 97 and 121 continue. The reason is not established by chain data.
- No native 4853 ballot from Vini’s board wallet.

### Rose

- 231 YES, nine NO, one ABSTAIN in 241 ballots.
- NO on A28, A59, A60, A106, A110, A205, A258, A272, and A288; ABSTAIN on A31.
- Rose supplied the only recorded NO on A272, causing it to fail with Vini absent.
- No proposal in the preserved A1–A289 set was authored by Rose.
- No native 4853 ballot from Rose’s board wallet.
- Treasury in the 2026 coverage window: **46,745.70 USDC**, including the change from 4,575 to 4,800-USDC recurring payments and 95.70 USDC.

- A159 included recurring payments to all four directors; Philipp, Vini, and Rose voted YES, supplying the exact 75-weight approval threshold. MB had no recorded ballot.
