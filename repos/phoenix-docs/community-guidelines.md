# Community Guidelines

## Accepting the Rules & Guidelines

Your presence in this forum implies accepting these rules, including all further changes. These rules are subject to change over time. It is your responsibility to verify if you're compliant at all times.

## Code of Conduct

In addition to these guidelines, all community members are expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/phoenix-directive/core/blob/release/v2.20/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which is based on the Contributor Covenant.

## Positively Contributing in Conversations

We want this space to be a strong asset for both teams building out the ecosystem and the community. In order to do so, all members should strive to be a positive impact in this space.

Constructive criticism is encouraged as long as it is clearly backed up by data or other sources of information. That being said, baseless criticism will be treated as FUD and is a bannable offence. This space is powered by you and your participation truly counts. Positively contributing in this forum will make Terra better for everyone.

## Be Civil

In order for this space to be as impactful towards the growth of the Terra ecosystem as possible, we ask that all community members participating be civil.

Some guidelines to follow:

- **Be respectful of others in the community:** toxicity, racism, misogyny etc. will not be tolerated.
- **No offensive content.** Please don't post anything that a reasonable person would consider offensive, abusive, or hate speech.
- **No obscene or sexually offensive posts.**
- **No incitement.** Encouraging the breaking of rules, inciting others to be blatantly rude and offensive, or otherwise promoting and/or encouraging conflicts between other members is not allowed.
- **No harassment.** There is no tolerance for community members harassing anyone else, impersonating people, or exposing other people's private information.
- **Don't post spam or otherwise vandalise the forum.**
- **No shilling/spamming,** including linking to discord servers.
- **No advertisements.** We do not tolerate any kind of advertisements, whether it be for other communities or streams, unless previously agreed upon with a staff member. You can post some content in relevant channels if it is relevant and provides actual value to others.
- **No impersonating of team members or mods/admins,** this will lead to an immediate ban.

These are by no means concrete rules with set definitions — avoid even the appearance of any of these above points. If you're unsure, ask yourself how you would feel if your message was quoted on the front page of Forbes.
