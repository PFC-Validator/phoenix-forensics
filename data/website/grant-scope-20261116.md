Editorial clarification dated 16 November 2026. This changes the presentation of existing evidence; it adds no fresh chain capture or legal ownership finding.

- TLA development #4813 and launch #4817: liquidity, voting and incentives infrastructure. The incorporated launch terms expressly promise migration-admin handover after one month and configuration-owner handover after three months.
- Phoenix formation #4816 and Treasury launch #4822: Treasury infrastructure, Board spending, vetoes, Alliance funding and published clawback protections. - The 16 November captures show deployed infrastructure, Board operating roles and Treasury veto configuration, alongside retained ERIS upgrade administration and registry ownership. Configuration verification is not exhaustive execution testing, a full grant-performance audit or certification of current code against a historical audit.
