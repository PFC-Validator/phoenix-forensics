No particular licence, source-code escrow, later-upgrade publication deadline or automatic penalty for withdrawing public access was identified in these reviewed texts. This is a bounded absence finding about the agreement texts, not a finding that the software has no licence or that no copy exists elsewhere.

The retained public repository check returned 404 on 16 November. That establishes inability to access the advertised location publicly at that check. It does not distinguish relocation from privatisation or establish the absence of Foundation/auditor access. Operational access, public-source availability, copyright ownership and on-chain upgrade authority are separate questions.

The earlier 2023 ERIS grant's “Full Open Source” wording occurs in its running-out-of-cash fallback answer. It is not imported as an unconditional, dated TLA/Treasury obligation.
